There is no show yet. No episodes, no backlog, nothing to binge. We could dress this page up to look like a network. We'd rather tell you the truth, because the whole point of this project is showing the work while it happens.

Here's what's real: a show was planned in the spring. Before the first episode shipped, the landscape moved out from under the plan. So it's being rebuilt for the way things stand now, not the way they stood then. If that sounds like the premise proving itself before the show even exists, it is. This is the window: the stretch of years where AI is powerful enough to reshape everything but the patterns aren't locked in yet. Decisions made now set the mold. That's what the show is about, and it's why we'd rather rebuild than ship stale.

When episode one exists, the people on this list will know first.
