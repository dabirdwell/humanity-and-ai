## What Is Clarity?

Clarity is a calmer reading experience built with dyslexia expertise. Not a summarizer: a reading environment designed around how people actually process text, especially those who've been told they're bad at it.

Clarity is a calm reading room. Make the text bigger, space it out, change the colors, have it read aloud, and tap any word for its meaning. No account needed.

## Key Features

- **A page you set yourself:** text size, spacing, line height, fonts, background colors and themes, all in the reader's settings.
- **Tap any word:** every word in the 217 library passages has a meaning you can tap, free.
- **Progress:** see which passages you have read and which words you are learning. It stays on your device.

## Pricing

The whole reader is free, with no account. Pro ($4.99 a month or $29.99 a year) adds plainer-words rewrites, a hard-words preview, summaries and more word help.
