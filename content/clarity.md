## Try it in one minute

1. Open [clarity.humanityandai.com/reader](https://clarity.humanityandai.com/reader) on any phone, tablet or laptop. A short reading is already on the page.
2. Tap any word. Clarity says it aloud and tells you what it means in that sentence.
3. Press **Listen** to hear the whole passage, at one of five speeds.
4. Press **Aa** on a phone, or **Settings** on a tablet or laptop, to make the text bigger, space it out, or change the background.

That is the whole idea. The reader sets the page, and help with any word is one tap away.

## Made for adults

Clarity began because so much reading help built with dyslexia in mind is made for children. Clarity is made for adults: readings on science, history, nature, government and daily life. Readers can also paste in their own messages, letters and articles.

## What is inside

- **217 short readings**, from easy to hard, on science, civics, nature, history and daily life. Most are in English; some are in French, Spanish and Portuguese.
- **A meaning for every word in those readings.** The meanings are built in, so a tap answers right away, even without a connection after a reading has been opened once.
- **The reader's own text.** Paste a message, a letter or an article and read it the same way. For their own text, a reader gets 10 word meanings a day, or 30 with a free account.
- **Practice rooms.** Letters: telling look-alike letters apart, like b and d. Words: see a word, then type it from memory. See and Hear: a passage read aloud, with each word lit as it is spoken.
- **A page that fits.** Text size, letter and line spacing, five reading fonts, and seven background colors. Each reader picks what feels easiest on their eyes. The colors are for comfort, not a treatment.

## For the children in your life

[Clarity Kids](https://clarity.humanityandai.com/kids) is a cozy room of 17 short stories in English, Spanish, French and German. A firefly named Lumen follows along as each story is read. A child taps a word to hear it and learn what it means. The English stories have a recorded read-aloud voice, and a grown-up can have it wait after each sentence to talk about a word.

It asks for nothing: no account, no email, no ads, no tracking. A child's progress stays on that device.

## Where it fits in your work

Clarity is a practice space, not a teaching program. It sits alongside what you already teach, including structured literacy (explicit teaching of how sounds, letters and word parts work). Between sessions, a reader practices on a calm page and can hear or look up any word without having to ask. One tap on a word keeps a reader moving. You decide when a reader should work a word out first.

- **For a client or student:** send them the link. There is nothing to install and no account to make.
- **On a phone or tablet:** open Clarity once on good Wi-Fi, then use the browser's Add to Home Screen option to put a Clarity icon on the home screen. After that it opens like an app, even without a connection.

## What Clarity stores and what it sends

- **No account is needed** to read, listen, look up words or practice.
- **Progress stays on the device.** If a reader makes an account, it is also saved there, so it follows them to another device.
- **Built-in meanings send nothing.** An AI model wrote them ahead of time, and they come with the reading, so a tap does not send the word anywhere.
- **Only two things reach an AI service (Anthropic).** A question about a word in the reader's own text sends that word and the text around it, to write the answer. Clarity's server keeps neither. The word is saved for practice on the reader's own device, or in their account if they have one. Pro's rewrites and summaries send the passage. A reader who would rather skip AI help can stay with the library readings.
- **Not a health record.** Clarity is a reading tool a person uses on their own. It is not built to hold health or therapy information, so leave session notes and records out of it.
- **No ads, and no selling or sharing of reading history.** The full [privacy policy](https://clarity.humanityandai.com/privacy) says exactly what is kept.
