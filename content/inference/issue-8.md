> **Correction, November 2026.** Three facts in this issue were wrong. In the bill numbers below, HB means a House bill.
>
> - **The count of AI bills.** This issue repeated Issue 7's claim that Oklahoma had "12 AI bills" passing by overwhelming votes. That count was wrong: Issue 7's list included three bills from 2024 and three bill numbers that did not belong to artificial intelligence (AI) bills. By April 18, 2026, seven bills whose official titles name AI had passed either the House or the Senate, and none had become law yet. [Issue 7](/inference/issue-7/) now carries a full correction.
> - **HB 2992's name and vote.** The issue called it the "Data Center Customer Protection Act" and said it "passed 92-2." Its name is the Data Center Customer Ratepayer Protection Act of 2026. The 92-2 vote was in the House only. The Senate passed it 46-0 on April 28, after this issue went out. Governor Stitt signed it on May 11, 2026, and it took effect July 1, 2026.
> - **Time left in the session.** The issue said seven weeks remained. The session had to end by May 29, six weeks later, and it ended on May 14.
>
> Every bill's record is on the Oklahoma Legislature's website, [oklegislature.gov](https://www.oklegislature.gov/).
