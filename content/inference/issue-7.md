> **Correction, November 2026.** This issue got several facts about Oklahoma's 2026 artificial intelligence (AI) bills wrong. In the bill numbers below, HB means a House bill and SB means a Senate bill.
>
> - **Three bills it listed were from 2024, not 2026.** HB 3453 (an "AI Bill of Rights"), HB 3828 (a list of the AI systems state agencies use) and HB 3577 (insurers disclosing their use of AI in claim reviews) were filed in the 2024 session. None became law, and none was before the Legislature in 2026. In 2026, those three numbers belonged to unrelated bills.
> - **Three more numbers did not belong to AI bills.** HB 2321, HB 2368 and SB 1444 were not bills about AI-made content or about AI posing as a mental health professional. In 2025 and 2026 they were bills about the state bar, corporations and insurance rates.
> - **Oklahoma had not passed "twelve AI bills" with "combined margins of 261-12."** By April 10, 2026, the day this issue went out, seven bills whose official titles name AI had passed either the House or the Senate, and none had become law yet: SB 1521, SB 1734, HB 1782, HB 3176, HB 3544, HB 3545 and HB 3546. We cannot match the 261-12 figure to any set of votes. Two of the four votes the issue offered as proof that lawmakers agree on AI were not on AI bills: the 92-2 vote was on HB 2992, about data centers' power costs, and the 85-6 vote was on HB 3173, about reusing old oil and gas wells.
> - **HB 1916 was filed.** The chart calls it "unfiled." It was filed for the 2025 session and sent to the House Rules Committee on February 4, 2025, which never gave it a hearing, as the article says.
> - **What else was true.** SB 894, the political ad bill the issue names, was a real 2025 bill, but it never got a vote of the full Senate. Three 2026 bills did take on AI in health insurance decisions: SB 2038, SB 1967 and HB 3675. None got a committee hearing.
>
> Every bill's record is on the Oklahoma Legislature's website, [oklegislature.gov](https://www.oklegislature.gov/).
